Rachid Karami International Fair · Tripoli, Lebanon

01A MODERNIST LANDMARK

An unfinished
vision.

The Rachid Karami International Fair is one of the most significant modernist architectural ensembles in Lebanon.

Commissioned in 1962 and designed by Brazilian architect Oscar Niemeyer, the fairgrounds were envisioned as a major civic and economic site in Tripoli. Construction was interrupted by the Lebanese Civil War, leaving much of the project unfinished.

The wider complex includes the experimental theatre known as the Dome, a place whose extraordinary acoustics later became central to The Dome Sessions.

02UNESCO WORLD HERITAGE

Recognized
in 2023.

In 2023, the Rachid Karami International Fair was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List and simultaneously added to the List of World Heritage in Danger. Both designations highlight the site's architectural significance and the challenges facing its conservation.

For detailed heritage documentation and conservation updates, consult UNESCO and the official fair authority.
